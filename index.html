Our story

A kennel built around dogs, not around business

When we set out to build Maesylan in 2019, we started with one question: what would we want for our own dogs? The answer became nineteen spacious suites with underfloor heating, private outdoor runs, a big exercise field and — because no one likes worrying from a distance — a live camera in every room.

Set in eight acres of countryside between Whitland and Cardigan, with views across to the Preseli Hills, Maesylan is licensed and inspected by Carmarthenshire County Council. Every guest is looked after by us personally — fed, walked, played with and, most importantly, made to feel at home.

  • We care for every dog ourselves — no large kennel teams, no shift handovers
  • Feeders, bedding and medicines follow your exact instructions
  • Happy to welcome nervous, senior and special-needs dogs
